Your neighbourhood barbers, since 2009.

We have been cutting hair in the centre of Hampton Vale since 2009. Some of our customers first came in as schoolboys and now bring children of their own.

Between us we have more than thirty years behind the chair. Bring a photo, or describe what you want, and we will take it from there.

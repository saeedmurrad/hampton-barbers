    First visit

    Good to know.

    Do I need an appointment?

    No. Hampton Barbers is a walk-in shop. Come in during opening hours and we’ll see you as soon as a chair is free.

    Do you cut children’s hair?

    Yes. See the price list for children’s cuts and the Father & Son offer.

    Can I show my barber a photo?

    Yes. Bring a reference photo or pick a style from our gallery and show it to your barber.

    Who can I ask about parking, access or payment?

    Call the shop before your visit and we’ll help you plan it.

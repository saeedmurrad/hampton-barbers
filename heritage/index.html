Part of Hampton since 2009

We opened in the heart of Hampton Vale in 2009. Plenty of the lads who had their first proper haircut with us now bring their own children in.

Our barbers have more than thirty years of experience between them. Skin fades, scissor cuts, cut throat finishes and beard trims. Show us a photo, or just tell us roughly what you are after.

Seven days a week. No appointment, ever.
